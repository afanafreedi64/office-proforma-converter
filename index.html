Office Proforma Converter

Offline-first document conversion prototype

1. Choose a document

Supported input: PDF, JPG and PNG. For this first prototype, text-based PDFs are supported best. Scanned documents require OCR and may need online or advanced processing.

Ready. Your file stays on this device unless you explicitly approve an online upload.

2. Online fallback

If local extraction cannot read your document, this prototype will not upload it automatically. Online conversion is not connected yet. A future online service must show its provider, privacy/retention terms and ask for your approval first.

Prototype limitations

  • Extracts selectable text from PDFs and creates a basic editable Word or Excel file.
  • Does not yet reconstruct complex tables, merged cells, borders, page layouts or handwriting.
  • Image OCR is not enabled in this first milestone.
  • Install/offline caching requires opening this app once while connected to the internet on an HTTPS host.
